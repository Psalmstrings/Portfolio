Project Overview

HireFit is an advanced, production-grade career technology platform built to address one of the most persistent bottlenecks in modern hiring: the disconnect between candidate resumes and specific employer expectations.

Rather than acting as a generic resume generator that creates synthetic, hallucinated career claims, HireFit is built on a Strict Zero-Fabrication Guarantee. It preserves the candidate's authentic employment history, education, and dates while parsing unstructured documents into structured schemas, scoring alignment across 6 evidence-based dimensions, and guiding candidates to reframe their real achievements in high-impact language aligned with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

02

The Real-World Problem

Job applicants and modern talent platforms encounter major friction when handling resumes:

✕ The Job Search Pitfalls

  • The Generic CV Trap: Sending the exact same CV to dozens of distinct roles without aligning keywords or highlighting relevant competencies.
  • AI Hallucination & Fabrication: Generic AI tools often invent job titles, companies, certifications, or statistics that candidates cannot defend during interviews.
  • Document Structure Destruction: Traditional resume optimizers discard the candidate's original layout, section ordering, and visual formatting.
  • Opaque Rejection Criteria: Candidates receive automated ATS rejections without knowing whether the gap was due to missing skills, poor phrasing, or formatting errors.

✓ The HireFit Engineering Approach

  • Zero-Fabrication Architecture: Hard-coded prompt constraints that strictly forbid hallucinating employers, credentials, or metrics.
  • 6-Category Multi-Dimensional Match Engine: Algorithmic scoring of Skills, Experience, Keywords, Responsibilities, Education, and ATS Readiness.
  • Structure-Preserving JSON Schema: Extracted resumes retain their precise section hierarchies, chronological orders, and formatting integrity.
  • Interactive Before/After Diff Inspector: Visual review pane enabling users to accept, reject, or fine-tune individual AI suggestions before committing.

Engineering Decisions & Ethical AI Design

1. The Zero-Fabrication Directive Ethical Engineering

Many AI resume tools hallucinate credentials to inflate match scores. When candidates are interviewed, these false claims collapse. In HireFit, I designed the system prompt to explicitly restrict the AI from introducing unverified companies, titles, degrees, or quantitative results. The AI is limited to restructuring real bullet points with stronger action verbs, highlighting genuine keywords, and clarifying context.

2. 'Not Evidenced' vs. 'Missing Skill' Taxonomy UX Decision

Traditional match engines tell candidates "You lack skill X". However, candidates often possess skills that simply were not written down. HireFit frames these as "Not Evidenced in CV", guiding candidates to add the experience if they possess it, or focus their cover letter on how they plan to acquire it.

3. Hybrid Cloud & Disk Storage Resilience Reliability

To guarantee zero downtime even during third-party cloud API outages, document uploads utilize a dual-path pipeline: uploading raw assets to Cloudinary while maintaining a secure disk fallback with automatic retry queues.
